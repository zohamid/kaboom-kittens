How to Play

In one line: take turns drawing cards, and don't be the one who draws a Kaboom Kitten. Last cat standing wins.

Your turn, step by step

  1. Play cards — as many as you like, or none at all. Attack, Skip, Favor, Shuffle, See the Future, or a cat combo. This part is completely optional.
  2. Draw one card. This ends your turn — even if you already played something. It's the step everyone forgets, so the game nudges you when you're standing there mid-turn.

That's the entire loop. Everything else is just the trouble you cause in step 1.

The moment of truth

If the card you draw is a Kaboom Kitten, you explode and you're out of the game — unless you're holding a Defuse. Play it and you survive. Then you secretly slide that kitten back into the deck anywhere you like: right on top for the very next player 😈, buried at the bottom, or somewhere in the middle. Nobody sees where it went.

The cards

Cat combos

Cat cards have no power on their own — they're only worth anything in matching sets.

Two matching cats

Steal a random card from any player. You don't get to choose which one.

Three matching cats

Name a card type — if that player has one, they must hand it over. Great for stealing a Defuse.

Five different cats

Take any card you want from the discard pile.

The cat families

Ten families exist, and each game deals a random five — so the cast changes every round. The log tells you who turned up.

Nope — the card everyone argues about

  • You may play a Nope at any time, even when it isn't your turn.
  • It cancels the card that was just played, as though it never happened.
  • A Nope played on a Nope becomes a "Yup" — the original card happens after all. They can stack as long as people keep having them.
  • You cannot Nope a Kaboom Kitten or a Defuse. Those always happen.

Attack — the mean one

Attack ends your turn immediately, without drawing, and forces the next player to take two turns. If they Attack back before drawing, it piles up: 2 turns, then 4, then 6… Someone eventually has to draw all of them.

How the deck is stacked

Everyone starts with 7 cards plus one guaranteed Defuse. The deck then holds exactly one fewer Kaboom Kitten than there are players — which is why the game always ends with exactly one survivor, and why someone will find one eventually.

Three ways to play

  • Vs Bots — you against up to four scheming robot cats. No setup.
  • Pass & Play — one device passed around a real table; a curtain hides each hand between turns.
  • Online — share a 4-letter room code and everyone plays from their own phone, with table chat 💬 and emoji reactions.

A few dirty tricks

  • See the Future, then Attack — hand the danger to somebody else without touching it.
  • Save a Nope for the moment someone Favors you when you're down to two cards.
  • Defusing? Putting the kitten on top is brutal — but if they hold a Defuse, you've just armed them and moved it right back toward you.
  • Watch the deck counter. Three cards left with two kittens still in there is a bad place to be.
