How to Play

The idea: take turns drawing cards. Hidden in the deck are Kaboom Kittens. Draw one and you explode — you're out! Unless you have a Defuse… The last player alive wins.

On your turn: play as many cards as you like (or none), then draw one card to end your turn. That's it — no kitten!

Setup: everyone starts with 7 cards + 1 Defuse. The deck holds one fewer Kaboom Kitten than there are players — so someone will find one eventually.

Noping a Nope turns it into a "Yup" — the original card happens after all. Nopes can stack forever.
